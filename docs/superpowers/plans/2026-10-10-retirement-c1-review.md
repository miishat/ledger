## Shared Interfaces

New `src/utils/finance/retirementTypes.ts` exports typed `WorkplacePlan`, resolved annual amounts, `TaxProfile`, year-tagged known/unknown capacity, `SalaryRetirementScenario`, snapshot provenance, validation and result branches. Canonical plan kinds distinguish group RRSP, DC RPP, DB RPP, RRSP/DPSP, PRPP and qualifying VRSP. Unknown DB funding and withdrawal restrictions remain explicit.

`retirementMatching.ts` resolves annual contracts with eligible base/bonus or explicit override. For each tier compute `ratio * Math.max(0, Math.min(employee, eligible * upper / 100) - eligible * lower / 100)`. Add unconditional dollars or eligible percentage, then cap combined employer funding. A null cap means no cap; zero means zero. Annual dollar inputs remain at zero eligible income.

`retirementEstimate.ts` consumes canonical cash pay, resolved workplace amounts, shared profile and personal inclusion. It rebuilds all derived earnings on each call. It returns branch-aware annual cash/tax/statutory totals, contribution/deduction allocation, room/PA/registered-limit warnings, contextual marginal rates and RRSP efficiency bounds. No consumer reimplements matching or tax classification.

The existing `annualSalaryTax(gross, province, rrsp, fhsa)` API remains behavior-compatible. Add an explicit earnings/deduction context for split CPP/QPP, EI, QPIP, employment-credit and Quebec worker bases and RPP deductions. Ordinary registered employer funding is not taxable; employer RRSP is taxable and pensionable, with EI/QPIP dependent on restrictions. Unresolved restrictions require restricted and withdrawable branches.

Planner owns typed shared profile, typed Salary & Tax scenario and preserved personal scalar amounts. Compensation owns independent primary/comparison plans and one personal-inclusion toggle. Legacy normalizers run through hydrate, backup restore, undo and Drive pull. Transferred snapshots replace canonical salary/plans atomically, preserve personal/profile values, record provenance and rebuild classifications after edits.
